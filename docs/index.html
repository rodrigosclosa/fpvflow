Gyro stabilization built for FPV pilots

FPVFlow is a free fork of Gyroflow that turns your FPV footage into a finished file in one pass: stabilized with the camera's own gyro data, colour graded with your LUT, with the external audio synced by itself - and with the DJI O4 false rotations removed.

New in 1.0.3

DJI O4 Gyrogate repair

O4 Air Unit Pro units built since early 2026, and every O4 Lite, record short false rotations under motor vibration. Stabilized, the video jerks and slowly sways. FPVFlow finds and removes them.

  • Tells which gyro your unit most likely has, from its serial number
  • Counts the false rotations in each clip and turns the repair on by itself
  • Removes each burst and the camera's slow pull-back that follows it
  • Saves a corrected _gyrofix copy that any Gyroflow opens

Colour grading with your LUTs

D-Log M or GoPro Flat need a conversion LUT before they look right. FPVFlow applies it while it stabilizes, so the export is already graded.

  • Any Adobe .cube LUT, 1D or 3D, with an intensity slider
  • A LUT library folder, two clicks away
  • 13 adjustments: exposure, contrast, highlights, shadows, white balance, saturation, vignette and more
  • 10-bit stays 10-bit; the output is tagged Rec.709

Color - D-Log M to Rec.709

External audio that syncs itself

A DJI Mic on the pilot, or a camera without a microphone: import the separate recording and FPVFlow lines it up with the video.

  • Finds the drone's arming beep and the moment the props stop
  • Handles recordings started by hand, before arming
  • Plays in the preview, with the waveform on the timeline
  • Embedded in the export, in its original format where possible

Stabilization

Smooth footage from the gyro, not from guesswork

The camera records its own motion. FPVFlow uses it to undo shake, roll and rolling shutter precisely, frame by frame.

Built on Gyroflow 1.6.3

Everything Gyroflow does, unchanged

FPVFlow adds to Gyroflow rather than replacing it. Its projects, presets and lens profiles work the same.

Real-time preview

Every adjustment shows up immediately, on the GPU.

Rolling shutter correction

Removes the jello that fast motion leaves in CMOS footage.

Lens profile database

Official profiles for GoPro, DJI, Sony, Insta360 and RunCam, updated automatically.

Smoothing algorithms

Several algorithms, horizon levelling and per-axis smoothness.

Adaptive zoom

Dynamic cropping keeps the frame filled with as little zoom as possible.

Keyframes

Change any setting over the length of the clip.

Render queue

Batch your flights and export them in one go.

Pro codecs, 10-bit

H.264, H.265, ProRes, DNxHD, CineForm and OpenEXR, GPU accelerated.

Video editor plugins

Stabilize directly in DaVinci Resolve, Premiere, After Effects or Final Cut Pro.
